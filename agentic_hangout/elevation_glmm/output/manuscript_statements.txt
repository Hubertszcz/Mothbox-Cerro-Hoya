Manuscript statistical statements (copy into Results)

1. Mean detections per photo decreased with elevation (p = 0.032).
2. Morphospecies richness decreased with elevation (p = 0.035).
3. Shannon diversity decreased with elevation (p = <0.001).
4. Simpson diversity did not vary significantly with elevation (p = 0.065).
5. Lepidoptera detections per photo did not vary significantly with elevation (p = 0.738).
6. Coleoptera detections per photo decreased with elevation (p = <0.001).
7. Hemiptera detections per photo decreased with elevation (p = 0.020).
8. Diptera detections per photo did not vary significantly with elevation (p = 0.370).
9. Session (hour) was not a significant predictor of Lepidoptera detections per photo (p = 0.091).
10. Lepidoptera: session was not significant (p = 0.195); elevation was not significant (p = 0.052); hour x elevation interaction was significant (p = 0.012).
11. Session (hour) was a significant predictor of Coleoptera detections per photo (p = <0.001).
12. Coleoptera: session was significant (p = <0.001); elevation was significant (p = <0.001); hour x elevation interaction was significant (p = <0.001).
13. Session (hour) was a significant predictor of Hemiptera detections per photo (p = <0.001).
14. Hemiptera: session was significant (p = <0.001); elevation was significant (p = <0.001); hour x elevation interaction was significant (p = <0.001).
15. Session (hour) was a significant predictor of Diptera detections per photo (p = <0.001).
16. Diptera: session was significant (p = <0.001); elevation was significant (p = 0.027); hour x elevation interaction was significant (p = 0.002).
